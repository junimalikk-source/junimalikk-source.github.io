The process

How importing works.

Importing a car should not feel like a gamble. Here is exactly how a car gets from a Japanese auction hall, or the UK trade, onto your driveway, with the paperwork to prove every step.

  1. 1

    You tell us what you want

    A specific car, or a budget and a feeling. We talk through grades, specs and what is realistic to find at the Japanese auctions or in the UK trade.

  2. 2

    We find and inspect it

    We bid only on cars we have seen graded. The auction sheet records every panel, mark and mechanical note. If it does not stack up, we walk away.

  3. 3

    Shipped and insured

    Roughly four to six weeks at sea, fully insured, photographed before loading in Japan and again when it lands in the UK.

  4. 4

    Customs and registration

    We handle import duty and VAT, the IVA test where it applies, and DVLA registration. The car arrives to you on UK plates.

  5. 5

    Prepared in Bradford

    A full service, fresh MOT, underseal and a careful going-over. Anything the auction sheet flagged is addressed and documented.

  6. 6

    You collect, with the file

    Every document from the journey comes with the car: auction sheet, export certificate, service record and V5C.
