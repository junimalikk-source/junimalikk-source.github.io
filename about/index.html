About

A small yard,
an exacting standard.

The Auto Yard started with a simple frustration: too many imported cars sold on a photo and a promise, with the real history left in Japan. We do it the other way round. We buy a handful of cars at a time, we keep the paperwork that proves what they are, and we let the facts do the selling.

Every car is bought against its auction sheet, the independent grade that records each panel and every mark. We verify the mileage against that sheet and the export documents before a car ever leaves Japan. Cars from the UK trade get the same scrutiny: full history, or we are not interested.

When a car lands, it is serviced, undersealed and MOT'd here in Bradford before it goes on sale. Then you come and see it, with the whole file on the table. No pressure, no moving prices.

Honest grading
